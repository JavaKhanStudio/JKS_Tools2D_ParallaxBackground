What this lab shows

The shader animations r375 proposed for water and clouds, written as they would ship (GLSL ES 1.0, sines only, in world units across the layer, so they run on across its tiles), drawn by the real reader compiled to JavaScript. None is in the library yet: pick here, then each one picked is written three times (libGDX, Godot, jME). The sea is r365's AI beach page, its sea a SHADER layer drawn four ways. WAVE is what ships today: whole rows slide sideways. SWELL: the water moved by two crossing travelling waves, sideways and up and down, so the painted crests bend. GLINT: sun glitter on the painting's own crests (r408): where a pixel is brighter than the water just above and below it, pieces of that line light up and fade in and out in place, each at its own moment, inside a sun's column across the view (its place and width are sliders). SWELL + GLINT: both in one shader, the swell's numbers the layer's, the glint's its own. SWELL + REFLECT (r419): the feeling of a reflection, not a real one: the far range drawn again as a layer over the swelling sea, flipped, at the range's own speed, its axis the sea's top line, broken into bands that slide sideways and thin out, faded with depth, drawn as a silhouette darker than the water (shade) and squashed so the range's peaks point down into the visible sea (squash). It would ship as one effect on a flipped IMAGE layer. SWELL + SHEEN (r419, after REFLECT): no second layer, the swelling sea itself mirrors the sky: broken lines across the water catch the pale sky at the horizon, others go a darker blue, and they run with the swell; strongest near the horizon, where water mirrors most (strength), fading to a fifth 2.5 world units under it. Streaks: how much of each slope catches the sky. It would ship as one effect on the sea, written three times, its sky the page's gradient. The clouds are round1's calm page, its clouds on a layer of their own (as a generated page will have them, r409), drawn still and under BILLOW, in the page and alone (the mountains in front left out): the clouds churn in place, a slow warp of their pixels. Their drift (the page's at rest speed) can be slowed to judge the churn alone. The view picks four panels or one effect close; zoom enlarges the frame (the panels stay sharp: it is drawn at that size). Amplitudes and wavelengths are in world units, the readout gives them in the image's pixels. Copy settings gives every slider as JSON for the card.
