Animation lab which shader animations ship?

What this lab shows

The shader animations r375 proposed for water and clouds, written as they would ship (GLSL ES 1.0, sines only, in world units across the layer, so they run on across its tiles), drawn by the real reader compiled to JavaScript. None is in the library yet: pick here, then each one picked is written three times (libGDX, Godot, jME). The sea is r365's AI beach page, its sea a SHADER layer drawn four ways. WAVE is what ships today: whole rows slide sideways. SWELL: the water moved by two crossing travelling waves, sideways and up and down, so the painted crests bend. GLINT: sun glitter, specks of light where three bent waves peak at once, only on the lit part of the water. SWELL + GLINT: both in one shader, the swell's numbers the layer's, the glint's its own. The clouds are round1's calm page, its clouds on a layer of their own (as a generated page will have them, r409), drawn still and under BILLOW, in the page and alone (the mountains in front left out): the clouds churn in place, a slow warp of their pixels. Their drift (the page's at rest speed) can be slowed to judge the churn alone. The view picks four panels or one effect close; zoom enlarges the frame (the panels stay sharp: it is drawn at that size). Amplitudes and wavelengths are in world units, the readout gives them in the image's pixels. Copy settings gives every slider as JSON for the card.
