What this lab shows

The shader animations r375 proposed for water and clouds, written as they would ship (GLSL ES 1.0, sines only, in world units across the layer, so they run on across its tiles), drawn by the real reader compiled to JavaScript. None is in the library yet: pick here, then each one picked is written three times (libGDX, Godot, jME). The sea is r365's AI beach page, its sea a SHADER layer drawn four ways. WAVE is what ships today: whole rows slide sideways. SWELL: the water moved by two crossing travelling waves, sideways and up and down, so the painted crests bend. GLINT: sun glitter on the painting's own crests (r408): where a pixel is brighter than the water just above and below it, pieces of that line light up and fade in and out in place, each at its own moment, inside a sun's column across the view (its place and width are sliders). SWELL + GLINT: both in one shader, the swell's numbers the layer's, the glint's its own. SWELL + MIRROR (r419, after REFLECT and SHEEN): the whole of the water is a mirror of the sky, as polished metal is of what is round it: flat, it mirrors the pale sky at the horizon, and the bluer sky over the view deeper down; the swell's slopes and the painted waves' own relief tilt it, so its facets go bright and dark as the swell moves. Strength: how much of the water is mirror. Relief: how much the painted waves bend it. Shine: hard facets, like metal, or soft. Tint: how much the water's colour tints what it mirrors. By the water's colour (r419, after Simon's 13:15 tuning): the painting keeps its own colour and a facet only bends it, lighter or darker by how much more or less light it mirrors than flat water there, so the dark and the pale water move alike; its panel + GLINT adds GLINT's sun glitter, by GLINT's sliders (its strength at 0: none). The view 'sea: MIRROR or not' sets SWELL, MIRROR as you tuned it (fixed at your numbers), and the two by the water's colour side by side. It would ship as one effect on the sea, written three times, its sky the page's gradient. The clouds are round1's calm page, its clouds on a layer of their own (as a generated page will have them, r409), drawn still and under BILLOW, in the page and alone (the mountains in front left out): the clouds churn in place, a slow warp of their pixels. Their drift (the page's at rest speed) can be slowed to judge the churn alone. The view picks four panels or one effect close; zoom enlarges the frame (the panels stay sharp: it is drawn at that size). Amplitudes and wavelengths are in world units, the readout gives them in the image's pixels. Copy settings gives every slider as JSON for the card.
