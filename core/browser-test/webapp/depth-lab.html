What this lab shows

A parallax speed is one over a distance: a layer at a quarter of the front layer's speed is four times as far from the camera. The speeds give every distance but one, the camera's distance to the front layer, which is the unit here ("4.0×": four front distances) and which the camera's view slider sets in world units. Each card is the real reader drawing that one layer, standing at its distance and scaled by it about the camera's eye: from the eye, it covers exactly what it covers in the game, so at 2D / 3D 0 the picture is the game's frame, and the slide to 3D is the same camera walking out. The ground is ruled at each front distance (true, or compressed for a deep page: the numbers stay true), each card's foot in its colour, the gap to the next card written beside it; the yellow lines are the camera's view. Drag the canvas to turn round the page, the wheel to come closer, the button or the space bar to slide between 2D and 3D. The layers keep scrolling at their speeds.
