Effects lab

What this lab shows

One lab per effect, and one with them all (r224). fog: the page fog (r217) mixes each layer toward the fog's colour by 1 - exp(-strength (1/speed - 1/front)), so the slower a layer scrolls the more it is fogged, the front layer never; the readout gives each layer's fog. snow: a PARTICLES layer's snow (the particles round's snow-wide.p): its density, flake size, wind, its place among the layers, its drift and its anchor. all: every effect that ships, together on the shaders round's s01: the page fog, the FOG mist, the trees' WAVE, a game's towers drawn in an EMPTY layer, and the snow; the right panel is the same page with none. floor (r275, in the reader since as a FLOOR layer, its front layers planted): a ground drawn as horizontal strips, its top row scrolling at the speed of the layer behind, its bottom row nearer than the trees, the rows between at the speed of their depth, each strip one plain image draw. Drag the picture, or hold the arrow keys, to pan. Mode 7 stretches the rows and spaces the image's rows by depth, so the ground recedes; line scroll only shifts each row, for art already painted in perspective. Every layer in front of the ground is planted: it scrolls at the speed of the ground row under its foot, as a thing standing on the ground does. Two parallax sets: Hiver on snow, and PurpleFairy on a violet ground; picking one puts the row speeds back to its own. tower (r276, r328): an image layer wrapped round a cylinder in the middle of the view, a brick tower in front of Hiver; the page's scroll turns it. Its radius, how much its wall darkens toward its sides, how fast it turns (its speed ratio) and how many images go round it are live; the readout gives the time a turn takes. planet (r277, r330, r347): the page on a turning world, every layer wrapped round one centre under the view, each turning at its own speed, what stands up leaning out from it; with a snowfall (a PARTICLES layer) and a game's towers (an EMPTY layer, drawn by a hook) among its layers. The reader turns each snowfall as one block, as the middle of its box turns; the hook asks the reader where a foot goes and how far it leans, and draws there: each tower on its own foot, its whole tile turned at once, or not at all. A radius of 0 is the flat page. lurker (r369): a hooded figure in a layer of its own behind Hiver's front pines, seen through their gaps. It may appear or vanish only while nothing of it is on screen: the cover, worked out once from the atlas, holds the offsets between the figure and each pine layer where one layer's opaque pixels hide it whole. The reader's sightings lets the reader switch it (r370): seen in one gap, gone in the next, back by its seed's draws, 1 in 3, each switch made while a trunk hides it or it is out of the view. Every 2 s, anywhere switches it on a timer, wherever it is: the pop the cover prevents. The strip is the last 20 s: green while a pine hides it whole, amber while it is in the view uncovered (which includes behind soft edges, or two layers together: the cover does not count those, so it never switches there), grey out of the view; white ticks are the switches. The readout counts the switches made while it was uncovered in the view: 0 in the first mode. The figure's speed and how often it comes by change nothing to the cover: it is worked out on the offset between the layers. Each page is drawn by the real reader, compiled to JavaScript, as a browser game draws it. The cost line gives the frame time and, per panel, its draw calls and the CPU time to submit them (the GPU's time is in the frame time).
