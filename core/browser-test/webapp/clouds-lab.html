What this lab shows

Candidate cloud shaders (r556), written as they would ship (GLSL ES 1.0) and drawn by the real reader compiled to JavaScript, in the three settings Ficelle needs first: a Mediterranean day, a night in a cold forest and a medieval fantasy evening. None is in the library yet. PUFFS (cumulus, round puffs heaped on a flat foot) and WISPS (streaks) are two shaders: the clouds are made in the shader, nothing is painted, so a page needs no cloud art; they are lit from where the sun or moon stands in the picture, in two colours a page would store (the lit and the shaded), which is all that changes from day to night. Painted, churned and relit is calm's painted clouds under BILLOW (a slow warp of their pixels), their colours replaced by the same two. Painted, still is what a page does today: the painting under the page's tint. The view picks PUFFS over WISPS in each setting, or one setting under every kind (PUFFS again in 3 flat bands, the toon way); a click on a panel zooms it over the stage. The sliders of PUFFS and WISPS scale each setting's own numbers, so one move shows in every setting; light turns the sun or moon round the sky, and the clouds' lit side follows. Copy settings gives every slider as JSON for the card.
