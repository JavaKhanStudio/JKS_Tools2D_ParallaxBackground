What this lab shows

The cloud animation r375 proposed, BILLOW, written as it would ship (GLSL ES 1.0, sines only, in world units across the layer, so it runs on across its tiles), drawn by the real reader compiled to JavaScript. It is not in the library yet: tune it here, then it is written three times (libGDX, Godot, jME). The clouds are round1's calm page, its clouds on a layer of their own (as a generated page will have them, r409). A churns them under BILLOW, B leaves them still, in the page; C and D the same with the mountains in front left out. BILLOW pushes each pixel of the clouds by a slow warp: amplitude is how far, wavelength how wide its swirls, speed how fast they turn. Their drift (the page's at rest speed) can be slowed to judge the churn alone. The view picks all four panels or two side by side; a click on a panel zooms it over the stage. Zoom enlarges the frame (the panels stay sharp: it is drawn at that size). Amplitudes and wavelengths are in world units, the readout gives them in the image's pixels. Copy settings gives every slider as JSON for the card.
