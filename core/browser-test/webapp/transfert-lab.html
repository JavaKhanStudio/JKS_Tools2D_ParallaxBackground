Transfert lab the cross-fade between pages, and the effects that can carry it

What this lab shows

The transfert is the library's cross-fade from one page into another (Parallax_Heart.transfertIntoPage): both pages keep scrolling, each layer slot fades from the old page's layer into the new one's, and the gradients fade too. Every panel runs that same transfert between the two pages the slider picks, back and forth; they only draw it differently. A is the library as it ships, in its depth stagger style (TransfertStyle.depthStagger, r249): each layer slot fades in its own window, back slots first; at stagger 0 every slot fades at once, the transfert a game gets without a style. B adds a colour grade a game can already do: the reader's tint (addColorTransfert) goes to the grade colour over the first half and back to white over the second; a tint multiplies, so it darkens and colours but never lightens. C is an effect this lab draws with its own shader, from FOG's noise: the mist's white rolls in in patches, far layers first, the page is swapped under the full fog, then it clears, near layers first. D is the library's dissolve (TransfertStyle.dissolve, r250): the new page's layers eat the old ones in patches, slot by slot, back slots first. E is the library's transfert through a colour (TransfertStyle.throughColor, r251): each layer slot's old layer is mixed toward E's colour until it is all that colour halfway through its window, then the new layer comes out of it, the gradients too; unlike B's tint it can go to white. The stagger slider spreads A, C, D and E over the depth, the library's own windows in all four; the patches and softness sliders shape C and D's mask. C is not in the library yet.
