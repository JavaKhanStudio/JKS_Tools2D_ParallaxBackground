What this lab shows

R1's AI forest page painted by dreamshaper_8 (ds8-forest: "misty pine forest in the mountains at dawn", seed 7), drawn twice by the real reader. Only its front pines differ between the two panels: the same painting, cut by today's key (layer.py) with its misty_pines rule on, as it ships since r400, or off. The rule keys out the pale, misty farther pines dreamshaper_8 paints at the forest's foot, behind the dark trunks: on, they and their bluish specks are gone; off, they stay, a depth cue. On this page it changes two of the front's four strips: front strip holds one of them still instead of the page's cycle, and marks lays red over every pixel the rule changes. Copy settings gives the choices as JSON for the card.
