The depth haze (r211) mixes every layer behind a hazy mist toward the mist's white, more for each layer further back (1 - (1 - haze)^layers). As it ships (A), a game's own drawing in an EMPTY layer (here: the dark towers) and a PARTICLES layer (the snow) stay as they are, sharp in front of a whitened hill; and when the page is tinted, the layers are tinted but the haze's white is not, so far layers drift back toward white. B hazes the towers and the snow like the images around them (in a game: one more shader switch for each such layer, and the Godot and jME readers ported). C mixes toward the mist's white times the page's tint. D does both. The haze, tint and scroll act on all four panels at once; the readout gives each layer's haze.
