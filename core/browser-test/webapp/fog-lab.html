FOG thins the mist's opacity by a sum of three sines. Across, they repeat every 1, 1/2 and 1/3 of a wavelength; up and down only every 2, 2 and 1.25 wavelengths, so today's patches (A) are 3 to 4 times taller than wide and read as vertical streaks on any band. B halves the heights: patches about as tall as wide. C is the same with a patch height a page would store (a fourth number, an editor slider). The scroll and every slider act on all four panels at once. The lab opens on Simon's r208 settings (shape A, amplitude 0.25, wavelength 8.25, no tree ripple); the mist's layer slider moves the mist in front of more layers. Depth haze mixes every layer behind the mist toward its white, more for each layer further back (1 - (1 - haze)^layers), in A to C; the plain panel has neither. The FOG itself only thins the mist picture, a band across the middle: without haze, nothing above or below it is fogged: the mist's size and up/down sliders grow and move that band. The parallax set slider puts the same mist into other pages (round1's calm, PurpleFairy and OneNight); every other slider acts on whichever set is shown.
