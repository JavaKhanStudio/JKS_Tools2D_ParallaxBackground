FOG thins the mist's opacity by a sum of three sines. Across, they repeat every 1, 1/2 and 1/3 of a wavelength; up and down only every 2, 2 and 1.25 wavelengths, so today's patches (A) are 3 to 4 times taller than wide and read as vertical streaks on any band. B halves the heights: patches about as tall as wide. C is the same with a patch height a page would store (a fourth number, an editor slider). The scroll and every slider act on all four panels at once.
